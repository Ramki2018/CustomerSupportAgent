# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the country. Express shipping takes
1-2 business days for an additional fee. Orders placed before 2 PM local time ship the
same business day; orders placed after 2 PM ship the next business day.

International shipping times vary by destination and customs processing, typically
7-14 business days.

Customers can track their order using the order ID once the item has shipped.

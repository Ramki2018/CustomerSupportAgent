# Return Policy

Customers may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund to
the original payment method, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.

The 30-day window is measured from the delivered date, not the order date, because
transit time varies and customers should get the full 30 days once the item is actually
in hand.

Digital goods, gift cards, and personalized/custom items are not eligible for return.

To start a return, the customer must contact support with their order ID. Support
representatives verify eligibility before issuing a return shipping label. This assistant
can check eligibility but cannot issue the label itself.
